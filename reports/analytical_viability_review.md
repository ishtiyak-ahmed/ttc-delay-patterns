# Analytical viability review — Task 5.2

## Decision

**REFRAME — retain the TTC data and pipeline, and sharpen the analytical question before Power BI.**

Recommended question: **Within each TTC mode, where do recorded incident frequency and recorded delay minutes point to different investigation priorities?** This is option B, proposed for review, not an implemented scope change. The existing question remains useful as an exploratory umbrella. Its word “recur” currently promises more temporal persistence than full-period rankings demonstrate.

The evidence is strong enough for a professional descriptive analytical case study alongside the engineering work. It is not yet a finished recurrence investigation. Two focused monthly cross-tabs can determine whether the strongest aggregate differences persist or are concentrated in particular periods. No new dataset, architectural change, code mapping or dashboard is needed for that decision.

## Minimum remaining work before Power BI — proposed, not executed

Existing outputs suffice for the bounded aggregate findings above. They do **not** establish temporal persistence or supply month-filterable route/code analyses. Recommend only two additional SQL result tables, followed by one short validation/review step:

1. **Mode × month × exact raw Code profile.** Counts, duration-eligible counts, zero counts, minutes, >60 and >120 duration-band contributions, and duplicate-candidate contributions. Use all eligible raw codes with a separately identified missing-code bucket, not just post-selected headline codes. Focus inspection on Bus MFDV/EFO and Subway MUIS. Compare monthly shares with the corresponding mode-month totals; determine whether aggregate concentration is broad or limited to a few months. Treat thresholds and duplicate flags as diagnostics, not exclusions or verified error labels.
2. **Mode × month × exact raw Line profile.** Counts, eligible-duration counts, zero counts, minutes and observed dates, with separately identified missing-line contributions. Inspect frequency-versus-duration profiles within mode across observed months. Do not normalize labels, pool modes, zero-fill absent reporting or use distinct observed dates as scheduled-service exposure. Determine whether a defensible route/line comparison adds to the main report; if not, keep route exploration in Power BI/appendix and replace the planned report page with stronger evidence.
3. **Reconcile and select claims, then define supported report filters.** Check both tables’ monthly count/minute/eligible-duration/zero totals against `monthly.csv` including missing buckets; respect the same explicit selection and source eligibility. Inspect how much headline conclusions rely on >120-minute or duplicate-candidate contributions using the first table and existing duration exports. Retain all records in primary measures. Report stability or concentration as actually observed, with no significance claim. A changed story is an acceptable result; it does not automatically justify more data or cleaning. State which claims survive and narrow the report accordingly.

No separate hour-by-month follow-up is required for the proposed main story: keep finding 5 supporting and aggregate-only. Weekday clarification, dictionary enrichment, geocoding, schedules, forecasting and significance modelling are not prerequisites for this bounded case study.

Power BI must respect aggregate grain: weighted mean = sum of eligible minutes / sum of eligible counts; zero share = summed zeros / summed eligible counts. Existing grouped medians and distinct-date counts cannot be recombined across arbitrary filters. Reuse the already-computed compatible median/coverage outputs or withhold those cards when filters exceed their supported grain. The two cross-tabs support month/category exploration but do not create arbitrary joint line-and-code filtering. An unsupported filter requires a later explicit export decision, not fabricated measures.

Completion gate: the focused outputs reconcile; claim wording reflects their actual evidence; coverage, missing categories and source selection remain visible; and supported filter/measure combinations are explicit. Then proceed to dashboard preparation after review. No broad research detour is needed.
